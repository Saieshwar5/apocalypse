                  Intercept a rock
                  Every rock heading for Earth carries a contract. Follow the yellow PRIORITY marker and the compass, get close, fire the laser; use missiles on iron and giants. Pushing a rock off course (a near miss) pays too and leaves less debris than shattering it. Early and clean pays a bonus.
                  Satellite tows
                  Fly to the satellite and match its speed, press T to connect, take it where the cyan diamond says (Vulcan Yard for repairs, back to its slot afterwards, clear of a rock's path for a dodge), nearly stop, press T to release. Pull gently: a fragile load is damaged by hard tugs.
                  Reboost
                  A satellite sinking into the swollen atmosphere: tow it back up to its slot before it burns.
                  Rock haul (Ceres)
                  Catch the marked carbon rock with the tow line and bring it to Ceres Works' intake. It is heavy: plan fuel and brake early.
                  Crew run (Halo)
                  Berth at Halo to take a crew aboard, hold beside the satellite while they work, then bring them home. Fly gently: hard hits hurt them.
                  Rescue
                  An escape pod falling towards the atmosphere: catch it with the tow line before it burns up and bring it to Halo's crew port.
                  Escort
                  Rocks will cross the supply rocket's climb: destroy or push them away before they meet it.
                  Sweep debris
                  Fly slowly through a debris cloud (amber rings) to net it, paid by the tonne. It keeps Orbit Health up.
                  Medals
                  Every job is graded bronze, silver or gold for how well it was done; silver and gold pay extra.
                  Target compass beside the crosshair. The dot is your objective: centre is dead ahead; towards an edge means turn that way. A hollow dot is behind you. Blue on the rim is Earth's side. Below: how far to turn, the distance, arrival time, and ▲▼ above or below you.
                  Steering arrow around the crosshair: the way to turn. It shrinks as the nose comes round and disappears ON TARGET.
                  Velocity marker: where you are really going relative to the objective. Put it on the target to meet it.

                  Flight director under the compass: one instruction at a time. TURN to the dot, BURN up to the speed shown, COAST until it says brake, BRAKE NOW, then MATCH SPEED. For moving targets a dashed route leads to a MEET HERE mark: where the target will be when you arrive.
                  Objective colours: priority rock, job, station, home (Aegis).
                  3D scanner (bottom left): your ship is the green arrow in the middle, nose up. Things stand on stalks: up means above you, a hollow end means below. Red/orange diamonds are rocks heading for Earth, faint grey dots are harmless rocks, squares are stations, triangles are rival pilots, dashed amber rings are debris, the pulsing ring is your objective. The faint wedge is what your cameras can see.
